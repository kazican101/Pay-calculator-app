Assumptions you should know. Unpaid meal breaks (default 30 min, clause 6.7) are deducted starting 2.5 hrs after shift start — so a 6am–6pm shift pays 11.5 hrs. Midnight-crossing shifts split at midnight for day rates and penalty windows. Weekday OT is tiered 150%/200% per shift; OT falling between midnight Friday and midnight Sunday pays 200%; OT on an RDO pays 200% with a 4-hour minimum. Shift penalties are not added on overtime hours, and public holidays pay 250% with no stacked shift penalty — the EBA doesn't state either rule explicitly, so confirm both against a payslip. Take-home is an estimate, not your payslip.
